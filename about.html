About MediTrack

MediTrack is a pharmacy stock and expiry management system. It helps a pharmacy sell medicines before they expire and order only what will actually sell.

The problem

Pharmacies often keep several batches of the same medicine with different expiry dates. When stock is sold without checking those dates, medicines expire on the shelf, and when reorders are guessed, the shelf either runs empty or fills with stock that never sells.

Our solution

MediTrack records every batch with its expiry date, sells the earliest-expiring batch first, warns the pharmacist about expiring and low stock, and suggests how much to reorder from past sales and supplier delivery time.

How it works

  1. Record batchesThe pharmacist adds each medicine with its batch number, quantity and expiry date.
  2. Sell the oldest firstAt the counter, each sale takes stock from the batch that expires first. An expired batch can never be sold.
  3. Get alerts and suggestionsMedicines close to expiry or running low appear in a list, along with a suggested reorder quantity.

What MediTrack takes care of

Batch-wise selling
Stock is tracked per batch, so the earliest-expiring stock leaves the shelf first.
Expiry and low-stock alerts
Problems show up in a list before they turn into a loss.
Smart reorder suggestion
Stock that will expire before it sells is not counted as usable when working out an order.
