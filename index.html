What MediTrack takes care of

Batch-wise selling
Each sale takes stock from the batch that expires first, and an expired batch can never be sold.
Expiry and low-stock alerts
Medicines close to expiry or running low show up in a list before they become a loss.
Smart reorder suggestion
Past sales and supplier delivery time decide how much to order. Stock that will expire before it sells is not counted as usable.
